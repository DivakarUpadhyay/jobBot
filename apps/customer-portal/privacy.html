Who else handles it

  • AI providers ({{AI_PROVIDERS}}): your resume, cover letter and the job description are sent to them to tailor each application and check that a job fits you. They process it on our instructions.
  • Your mail provider (for example Google or Microsoft): applications are sent through your own mailbox.
  • Payment providers (for example Razorpay or Stripe): they take the payment. We never receive card details.
  • Hosting: {{HOSTING}}, where the service and its backups run.
  • Employers: each application you send goes to the employer's address, with your resume attached.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. Where the law requires, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account, resume and applications: while your account is open. When you delete your account they are removed at once from the live service, and from backups within {{BACKUP_DAYS}} days as old backups are replaced.
  • Payment records: kept for the period tax law requires (in India, 8 years), without your name or email once the account is deleted.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Plan and billing → Download my data gives you everything we keep, as one file.
  • Correct it: edit your profile, resume and cover letter in the portal at any time.
  • Delete it: Plan and billing → Delete my account.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect your mailbox, switch the bot off, or delete your account. For a mailbox connected by signing in, you can also remove our access in your Google or Microsoft account.
  • Complain: to us first; you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your local data protection authority if you live in the EU or UK.

Using these rights is always free.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes; mailbox passwords and sign-in tokens are encrypted. Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Each account sees only its own data. Backups are made daily and checked. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Cookies and storage

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in token in its local storage until you sign out.

People who receive applications

If you received an application sent through JobPilot and do not want to hear from our users again, reply with "remove me" or write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Your address goes on a do-not-contact list that every account obeys.

Children

JobPilot is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will tell you by email or in the portal before a change to this policy takes effect.
