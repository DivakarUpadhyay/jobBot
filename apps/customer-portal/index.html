About us

Built for job seekers who value quality over spam

Mass-applying hurts your reputation with recruiters. JobPilot was built the other way round: fewer, better applications, each one written for the role from your real experience and sent from your own address, so replies come straight to you.

We serve candidates in every field, from software and data to healthcare, finance, teaching and operations, starting in India and expanding across Asia and the Middle East.

Our accuracy promise

✓Role match first. A job is kept only when its title is one of the roles you chose.
✓AI fit check. Before anything is written, the AI compares the job with your resume and drops jobs from another field or far above your level.
✓Nothing invented. Tailored resumes and emails use only facts from your resume and cover letter; anything new is flagged.
✓You stay in control. Approve each email, or let a paid plan send automatically within safe daily limits.
